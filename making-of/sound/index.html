Rather than carry 2016's sound files over, the port worked out what each sound did, and when, and then built every one of them again in the browser from oscillators and filtered noise. Nothing you hear in the game is a recording. Every sound is a short recipe in the source, so there's no file to license.

When each sound plays

The part that had to be ported faithfully was the rules: which sound starts on which event, which stops, and which fades under which. In 2016 the engine isn't a smooth curve at all. There's no pitch change anywhere in the original's own code, and what sounds like an engine note is a handful of loops swapped on and off at fixed speeds.
