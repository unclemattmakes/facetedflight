Porting Faceted Flight meant deciding, over and over, whether something it does is a bug or part of how it feels. Every one of those calls gets a row in the deviations ledger. Here are two that went opposite ways: something 2016 did that the port got wrong at first and had to put back, and a 2016 bug the port fixed.

The out-of-bounds countdown

In 2016 the play area is a sphere, and it's a trigger. Leave it and a countdown starts, which runs for on every shipped mission, and then the run restarts. Turn back inside and it cancels. (The C# source disagrees with the scenes about how long. The scenes are what shipped.)

The problem was in how Unity reports leaving. It fires an exit event for something that was inside and went out. A ship that spawns outside the sphere was never inside, so there's no exit to fire, and nothing happens. The port's first version asked a different question every tick: are you outside right now? Those two questions agree almost everywhere. They disagree on the two missions that spawn outside their own play area, where the countdown started on the first frame and reset the run again and again, forever. Two of the thirteen missions were unplayable, and none of the unit tests caught it, because every one of them started the ship in the middle of the sphere. An automated test that flies all thirteen missions in a browser is what found it.

A platinum nobody could earn

      

        rookie_01_hoverJet has a platinum threshold that sits above the most its rings
        can pay out, so nobody could earn it. That one was an arithmetic bug and not part of the
        feel, so it's fixed: Anniversary puts platinum at the ring ceiling. The rule lives in the
        data, and the toy reads it.

What stayed

      

        Some of 2016's quirks stayed because they're part of how it flies. The HoverJet still
        doesn't level itself out when you let go of the stick, same as in 2016. Each of these has a
        row in the deviations ledger saying what it was and why it went the way it did, so if one
        changes later there's a record of the reason.
