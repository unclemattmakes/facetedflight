Most of how the HoverJet feels comes from a few numbers on the ship and one decision about gravity.

Gravity, and when it switches off

The world's gravity is , not Earth's. Every mass, drag and thrust in the game was tuned against it. The HoverJet's prefab even says it uses gravity. But once the launch elevator finishes, the game switches gravity off for the rest of the flight, so a HoverJet in the air is just thrust against drag. A jet that hasn't launched yet still falls.
